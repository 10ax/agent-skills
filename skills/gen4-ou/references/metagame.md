# DPP OU metagame (August 2026)

## Tiering timeline

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| Jan 2025 | **Machamp banned** (No Guard + Dynamic Punch), 76.5% survey support |
| 5 Jan 2026 | **Sleep banned** — 22/36 council votes, clearing the 60% threshold |

The sleep ban is the load-bearing fact. A contributor in the metagame discussion thread calls it
*"the most impactful tiering action since Mence's ban."* Anything written before January 2026 —
including all sample teams and the Viability Rankings — must be re-read in that light.

What the ban covers: Spore, Sleep Powder, Hypnosis, Sing, Yawn, Lovely Kiss, Grass Whistle.
What it does **not** cover: **Rest and Sleep Talk**. RestTalk Rotom-H and Suicune still work.

Read the deltas, not just the ranks: Clefable 16→5, Hippowdon 32→22, Skarmory and Rotom-W both up,

semi-stall**, because sleep was the main thing keeping fat teams honest.

**Note for teambuilding:** Lucario (12%) + Infernape (9%) means roughly a fifth of ladder teams
carry a strong Fighting-type. A team with no Fighting answer is not a ladder team.

## Current threat discourse

From the metagame discussion thread, through July 2026:

- **CM Clefable with Flamethrower + Thunder Wave** is the consensus top threat — *"counters
  literally everything."* The reliable answer is **Seismic Toss**, which ignores Calm Mind boosts
  entirely. Taunt and Knock Off are the supporting pressure.
- **Jirachi's Iron Head** flinch + paralysis variance draws steady complaints. Hard counters exist
  (Heatran, Magnezone, Skarmory, Empoleon) but the RNG is considered unpleasant.
- **Thunder Wave saturation** — paralysis spam is widely called oppressive, especially paired with
  CM Clefable. Remember Electric-types are *not* immune in Gen 4.

Emerging tech being explored: Toxic Tyranitar to pressure defensive Latias and Starmie, Bold
Reflect Starmie, Life Orb Starmie and Aerodactyl, Donphan + Magnezone/Magneton trap-and-hazard
cores, Choice Specs Porygon-Z against fat teams.

## Archetypes

From [DPP OU Sample Teams v2](https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/dpp-ou-sample-teams-v2.3767939/)
(July 2025). **Its "Pre-Sleep Ban Samples" section — seven Breloom/Roserade teams — is now
obsolete.** The rest remain legal and current.

- **Hyper offense** — Azelf suicide lead + multiple Dragon Dance/Swords Dance sweepers. Underused
  right now, which makes it well positioned against the fat teams the sleep ban encouraged.
- **Bulky offense** — Latias/Tyranitar/Metagross cores with a trapper and one win condition.
- **Hazard stack offense** — suicide Skarmory lead, Spikes, offensive spinblocking.
- **ZapMie balance** — Zapdos + Starmie + Tyranitar + Jirachi, the classic four, plus two.
- **Magnezone/Magneton balance** — trap the opposing Steel so Latias and Jirachi stop being walled.
  Currently the best-positioned archetype.
- **Semi-stall** — Hippowdon/Skarmory/Clefable/Jirachi/Rotom-H, hazards plus phazing.
